## Decisions hardened in R2

Beyond what the master plan and R1 stated, R2 hardened:

1. **Five charter principles, not one finalist.** v0.3.0's design space is *compositional*, unlike v0.2.0's two-finalist split (PR-as-product / CMS-publishing). The charter is one-and-only-one, parameterised by substrate adapter.
2. **Voice is a per-adapter parameter, not a charter principle.** P6 evaluator (70/100) and EXPLORING regime confirm voice belongs inside the adapter's prose-layer template, not at the top-level charter. The architectural impact: `Adapter.voiceProfile: 'clinical-and-cited' | 'conversational-and-anecdotal' | 'scripted-and-timestamped'` is a per-adapter constant.
3. **Negative principle `inverted_retrieval_target` is enforced as a guard.** `pull_recommend` MUST reject recommendations that propose link-building / sitemap firehoses / IndexNow ping campaigns. This is not just a stance — it is a concrete check in `pull_recommend`'s post-validation, and Buddhi gate G1 enforces it.
4. **Three new Buddhi gates** (G1 charter-principle adherence; G2 voice-layer protection; G3 signal-strength sufficiency). Each fires at a specific MCP-tool boundary; each has an explicit failure envelope (no silent pass-throughs).
5. **Iteration-keyed Sublation.** Pratyakṣa store entries now carry an `iteration` integer. Four new sublation conditions: iteration retraction; anchor retirement; voice-change reversal; substrate-prior override. Each is a *Blessing in Disguise* (TRIZ #22) — failure is the audit trail the next iteration uses.
6. **Eight Sākṣī invariants for `/pull:*` sessions** (the v0.2.0 four + v0.3.0 five). The hook in `plugin/hooks/aeo-sakshi.sh` is updated in R5.
7. **R7 use-case U2 (Substack) is the most-likely candidate** to drive through the full DRAFT → ANALYZED loop because Substack's edit-and-republish cycle is shortest among the three substrates. (R7 is not committed to U2 — but the analysis says it is the lowest-friction path.)
